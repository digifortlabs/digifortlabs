*   **Tagline**: `SECURE HYBRID ARCHIVING`
*   **Compliance Badge Text**: `🛡️ DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANT`

### 2. Main Pitch & Headline
*   **Primary Headline**: **Is Your Lab Ready for the DPDP Act 2023?**

## ⚙️ Professional Desktop Publishing Specs

Before sending the flyer design to print, ensure the canvas complies with standard offset printing guidelines:

*   **Size**: A4 Portrait (**297mm x 210mm**)
*   **Bleed Boundaries**: Add **3mm** of bleed on each edge (Design canvas size: **303mm x 216mm**)
*   **Safe Margins**: Keep all critical text, icons, and logo borders **5mm inside** the trimmed boundary to prevent machine crop offsets.
*   **Color Profile**: Ensure target export is **CMYK** (FOGRA39 profile recommended).
*   **Resolution**: Set document raster assets to **300 DPI** minimum.
*   **Paper Stock**: **250 - 300 GSM Silk cardstock** with a Matte laminate finish (feels substantial and luxury).
