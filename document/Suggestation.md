# DigifortLabs HMS - Feature Suggestions

> [!WARNING]
> **Status: Not Approved**
> These are draft ideas and suggestions for future enhancements. None of these features have been approved for development yet.

Based on the comprehensive documentation of the DigifortLabs HMS, here are several advanced feature suggestions to make the system a "Next-Generation" Hospital Management System.

### 1. Specialized Departments (Currently Missing)
* **Blood Bank Management Module:** End-to-end tracking of blood donations, donor screening, component separation (PRBC, FFP, Platelets), cross-matching, and stringent expiry tracking.
* **Ambulance & Fleet Management:** GPS tracking of hospital ambulances, driver dispatching, paramedic emergency logs, and automated billing for transport services.
* **Dietary & Kitchen Management:** Directly linked to the IPD. Doctors prescribe a diet (e.g., Diabetic, Liquid, Low-Sodium), and the system auto-generates meal plans and kitchen prep lists for each ward, complete with kitchen inventory tracking.
* **Asset & Biomedical Engineering Maintenance (CMMS):** A module to track all medical equipment (MRI machines, ventilators, monitors). It tracks Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMCs), preventive maintenance schedules, and logs equipment downtime.

### 2. Advanced Patient Experience & Engagement
* **Patient Mobile App / Portal:** A dedicated portal where patients can view their E-prescriptions, download lab/radiology reports, track their vital history, book appointments, and pay outstanding bills.
* **Smart Queue Management System (QMS):** Integration with waiting room TVs (Digital Signage) displaying current token numbers, coupled with automated WhatsApp/SMS alerts saying *"You are next in line for Dr. Smith"* to prevent crowded waiting areas.
* **Self-Service Kiosks:** Touchscreen kiosks in the hospital lobby for repeat patients to quickly book OPD appointments, print their tokens, or pay bills without standing in the reception line.

### 3. AI & Clinical Decision Support (CDSS)
* **Drug-Drug Interaction & Allergy Alerts:** When a doctor prescribes a medication, the system automatically cross-references the patient's known allergies or current medications and flashes a warning if there is a severe interaction risk.
* **Voice-to-Text for Doctors:** Integration with medical speech-to-text AI (like Nuance/Dragon) so doctors can dictate OPD notes and surgical notes instead of typing them.
* **IoT Device Integration:** Integrating the ICU patient monitors directly into the HMS so that vitals (Heart Rate, SpO2, BP) are automatically plotted onto the nursing eMAR chart every hour without manual data entry.

### 4. Operations & Supply Chain Enhancements
* **RFID/Barcode Tracking for High-Value Implants:** For the OT module, using RFID to scan expensive orthopedic implants or cardiac stents to ensure zero revenue leakage and perfect batch traceability.
* **Vendor Management Portal:** A self-service web portal for pharmaceutical and consumable suppliers to view Purchase Orders, acknowledge them, and upload invoices directly into your system.
* **Laundry & Linen Management:** Tracking the daily movement of bedsheets, OT scrubs, and gowns to the laundry, monitoring wash cycles, and tracking linen depreciation/loss.

### 5. Advanced Billing & Financials
* **Patient E-Wallet / Smart Cards:** For long-term IPD patients or corporate clients, allowing them to top-up a prepaid hospital card/wallet that can be swiped seamlessly at the pharmacy, cafeteria, and billing desks.
* **AI Claim Denial Prediction:** For the TPA module, an AI checker that scans the bill against GIPSA rules *before* submitting it to the insurance company, warning the billing team if the claim is highly likely to be rejected due to missing paperwork.
