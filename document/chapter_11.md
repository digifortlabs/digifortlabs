# Chapter 11: MTP Act Compliance & CARA Child Adoption Registry

## 11.1 Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP Act 2021 Compliance)

The MTP Module governs confidential clinical workflows for pregnancy termination under the **Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) Amendment Act 2021** (India).

### 11.1.1 Gestational Age & Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) Opinion
- **Statutory Gestational Limits:** Enforces statutory RMP opinion sign-offs based on pregnancy duration:
  - **Up to 20 Weeks:** Requires 1 Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) opinion sign-off.
  - **20 to 24 Weeks:** Requires 2 RMPs opinion sign-offs for specified legal categories (*survivors of sexual assault, minors, change of marital status, severe fetal malformations*).
  - **Beyond 24 Weeks:** Requires State-level Medical Board approval for substantial fetal abnormalities.

### 11.1.2 Form C Confidential MTP Register
- **Encrypted Privacy Vault:** MTP procedures are recorded in an encrypted **Form C Register**. Patient legal names are anonymized on general hospital dashboards and stored under encrypted reference IDs.
- **Monthly CMO Reporting:** Auto-generates monthly statutory MTP reports sent to District Health Authorities / Chief Medical Officer (CMO).
- **MTP Drug Control:** Dual-authentication dispensing logs for Mifepristone & Misoprostol kits, tracking batch numbers and RMP prescription approvals.

## 11.2 CARA Child Adoption & Abandoned Newborn Registry

Complies with **CARA (Central Adoption Resource Authority) Guidelines** and the **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act 2015**.

### 11.2.1 Abandoned & Surrendered Newborn Protocol
- **Immediate Logging & 24-Hour Intimation:** Immediate logging of abandoned or surrendered newborns in hospital wards, triggering mandatory automated 24-hour notification logs sent to the local Child Welfare Committee (CWC) and police station.
- **DNA & Medical Fitness Records:** Stores DNA sample vouchers, pediatric medical fitness reports, and initial immunization history.

### 11.2.2 CARA Guidelines & Non-Commercial Audit
- **Legally Free for Adoption Tracking:** Tracks child status progression until declared "Legally Free for Adoption" by the CWC, coordinating digital handover to authorized Specialized Adoption Agencies (SAA).
- **Non-Commercial Adoption Audit:** Immutable audit trail enforcing strict legal prohibition of commercial adoption transactions.
