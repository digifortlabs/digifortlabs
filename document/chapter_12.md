# Chapter 12: Organ Donation, Transplant Registry & Cold Ischemia Telemetry

## 12.1 Brain Death Certification Protocol (THOA Compliance)

The Organ Transplant Module governs living and deceased organ donation workflows in strict accordance with the **Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOA 1994 / 2011 Rules)** and **NOTTO** guidelines.

### 12.1.1 4-Doctor Medical Board Sign-Off
- **Mandatory Panel Dual-Certification:** Enforces digital dual-certification of Brain Stem Death by a 4-member authorized medical panel:
  1. Medical Superintendent / Hospital In-Charge.
  2. Treating Physician / Intensivist.
  3. Neurologist / Neurosurgeon.
  4. Independent Authorized Doctor from the State Panel.

### 12.1.2 Apnoea Test Protocol Logging
- **2-Stage Apnoea Test Logs:** Mandates recording of **2 apnoea tests performed at least 6 hours apart** (logging baseline $PaCO_2$, 100% $O_2$ administration via endotracheal catheter, absence of respiratory efforts, and post-test $PaCO2 ≥ 60 mmHg$ or $20 mmHg$ increase above baseline).

## 12.2 Organ Retrieval, Matching & Cold Ischemia Telemetry

### 12.2.1 Recipient Matching & HLA Typing
- **Cross-Matching Engine:** Cross-matches donor HLA typing (HLA-A, B, DR), ABO blood group, body weight, geographic location, and waiting list urgency score against state/national recipient waiting lists.

### 12.2.2 Cold Ischemia Time (CIT) Telemetry
- **Real-Time CIT Countdown Timers:** Tracks maximum allowable organ cold ischemia limits from organ cross-clamping during retrieval to vascular un-clamping during recipient implantation:

$$\text{Heart (4–6 Hours)} \quad \vert \quad \text{Lungs (4–8 Hours)} \quad \vert \quad \text{Liver (12 Hours)} \quad \vert \quad \text{Kidneys (24 Hours)}$$

### 12.2.3 Statutory NOTTO / SOTTO / ROTTO Reporting
- **Statutory Transplant Notifications:** Auto-generates statutory organ retrieval and transplant outcome notifications sent to SOTTO (State), ROTTO (Regional), and NOTTO (National) registries.
